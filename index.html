About the numbers

Figures come from the Home Office's daily small boat activity page. They are provisional operational data and can be revised, including downwards. Finalised figures appear in the quarterly Immigration System Statistics.

Counts are people detected arriving in small boats (dinghies, RHIBs, kayaks). They exclude arrivals hidden in vehicles or on larger vessels, and anyone who arrived undetected. An uncontrolled landing is a boat that reached the shore without being intercepted at sea.

The daily figure for a given day is usually published the following morning, so "latest day" is normally yesterday.

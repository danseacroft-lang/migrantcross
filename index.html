      How the figures are released

      The Home Office publishes each day's figures the following morning, usually by about 10–11am. Weekend figures sometimes arrive on Monday.

      This site checks for new figures every 30 minutes from about 8am to 7:30pm, so new figures normally appear here within half an hour of being published.

      The figures are provisional. The Home Office sometimes corrects a day later; corrected days are marked in the calendar. Final checked figures come in its quarterly statistics.

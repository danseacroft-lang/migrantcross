    At weekends it can take several hours for the Home Office to release the figures, and it's not unusual for them to come out on the Monday instead.

    Between 12:00 and 15:30 some parts of the Channel Crossings site may become unavailable whilst our servers download the updated figures from the Home Office. This can take several hours.
